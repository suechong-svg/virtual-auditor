# Virtual Auditor (prototype)

An AI staff auditor for Internal Audit fieldwork. **Synthetic data only.**

- Reads auditee sources (read only) and works in the Audit workspace.
- Pauses for your approval before filing evidence, workpapers, issues or minutes.
- Every action is logged to `audit_workspace/_agent_activity_log.jsonl`.
